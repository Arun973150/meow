# add a slide in PowerPoint

when: powerpoint, slide, slides, presentation, deck, new slide, add slide

New Slide is on the Home ribbon at the far left, next to New. The ribbon has to
be showing first — if it is collapsed, the tab names are still visible and
clicking Home opens it.

The slide that appears has two placeholders. The top one is the title and is
usually named "Title 1" or "Title Text Box"; the one under it is the body and
is named "Content Placeholder" or "Subtitle". Click the placeholder before
typing, because typing with the slide selected but no placeholder focused goes
nowhere and reports success.

Use Layout, next to New Slide, to change an existing slide rather than deleting
and re-adding it.
